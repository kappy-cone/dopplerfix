# U19: detection arms (plan §8.2), session 6

## Step 1: the arms

Built in `doppler/detect.py`, off by default (V0 / V1 unchanged), registered in `reports/apriori_registry.md`
before any measurement:
- **(a)** a harmonic-summed tonal row: per frame, the max over fundamentals of the mean excess at f, 2f, 3f;
- **(b)** the prop bands on a 3 s long-window spectrogram;
- **(c)** a separate 20–100 Hz row, below the recording high-pass corner.

No existing threshold moved. Each arm only adds rows to the max-over-rows statistic.

## Step 2: detection-only screen (seeds 0–49)

**Method.** `reports/experiments/u19/screen.py` (summary: `summarize.py`). Each unit rebuilds the scenario as
`cli.py ablate` does, then runs `cli.run_events` (detection, ridge, fit; no cascade) once per arm on the same
audio, so every comparison is paired by seed and by audio.
- **Recall** is the Stage A definition: a truth event counts if a fitted event lies within 10 s of its received
  CPA time.
- **Spurious** = fitted events that match no truth event.
- Configs: clean, all-on, `loo:path`, `loo:levels`, `loo:scene`, `sub:reflection` (§8.4), seeds 0–49 each.
- It is a screen: rows stay in `outputs/`, not committed.

**Checks.**
- The base arm reproduces the detection counts recorded in the Stage A and U18 rows on all 125 overlapping
  units.
- The base arm after the arm code went in matches the base screen run before it on all 300 units, every field.

**Reading.**
- **Arm b (3 s long window) is the only arm with a clear effect.** It gains 7 all-on events and loses none
  (p = 0.016), with similar gains in `loo:path` (+13) and `sub:reflection` (+10). Spurious events do not go up:
  all-on 0.52 → 0.42 per run, 2 runs up and 6 down.
- **Arm a (harmonic sum) does nothing measurable** on all-on. On the test fixture its gain was only +1.5 dB:
  the realism layer's harmonics fall at −3 dB per order, so the mean over three harmonics is weaker than the
  fundamental.
- **Arm c (low band) helps only `loo:path`** (+6). Adding it to b gains nothing over b alone (abc ≈ b).
- **Where the gains come from.** All of b's all-on gains are events that never got a window: 5 below the

  event) is recovered. They are now the largest all-on loss and lie outside §8.2's candidates. They need a
  ridge / fit look.
- **Effect on accuracy.** Recall only bounds accuracy. More detections also mean more hypotheses and timeouts;
  only the decision run can show whether V1's rank-1 accuracy rises.

## Brainstorm after the screen (session 6): what the screen adds to `docs/u19_detection_literature.md`

The literature note's D1–D5 stand. These points come from the screen rows and the U18 rows, with no
re-run, and sharpen them.

**Findings**
- **F1. The cliff sits at about 6 dB band SNR.** Recall by the event's peak band SNR, pooled over the six
  configs:

  | band SNR (dB) | < 1 | 1–2 | 2–3 | 3–4 | 4–6 | 6–8 | ≥ 8 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | base | 0.52 | 0.59 | 0.69 | 0.66 | 0.67 | 1.00 | 0.97 |
  | arm b | 0.64 | 0.68 | 0.70 | 0.77 | 0.76 | 1.00 | 0.98 |

  322 of the 600 events lie below 6 dB, because the level model resamples events to ≥ 0 dB
  (`realism.event_min_snr_db`). Recall on the synthetic mix therefore mostly measures the generator's SNR
  distribution. **The transferable quantity is the recall-against-SNR curve**, which can be compared with
  field recordings.
- **F2. Some lost-after-detection misses look mis-timed, not lost.** Of the 14 all-on events that got a window
  but no matched fit, 6 sit in runs that also have a spurious fit: probably a fit whose t_cpa is more than 10 s
  from the truth (D1 cause 3). The other 8 have no fit near them at all (causes 1, 2, 4 or 5).
- **F3. Detection caps accuracy less than recall suggests.** On U18's all-on C n = 100, V1 has the right answer
  in 31 of the 65 fully detected runs, but in only 3 of the 35 runs that missed an event. **Perfect detection
  would lift V1's rank-1 accuracy (status ignored) from 34 to about 48 at most**, if those runs then behaved
  like the fully detected ones. Arm b recovers 7 of 25 all-on events, so expect a few runs, which a paired test
  at n = 100 cannot confirm. The acceptance rule should not hinge on a rank-1 gain. Discrimination (34 of the
  65 fully detected runs still fail) remains the larger lever.

**Additions to D1–D5**
- **E1. Oracle-window ladder (reporting only).**
  - Give the ridge and fit stage the truth event windows as manual windows. This is a diagnostic: never a run
    claim, and recorded as a manual input.
  - Then give the fit truth-derived ridge points (`tests/test_cascade.ridge_events`).
  - The two rungs bound what detection fixes (D3) and ridge fixes (D2) can achieve, and finish D1's breakdown
    in one screen.
- **E2. Carry arm b into the ridge stage.** Arm b only adds detection rows. The ridge DP still runs on the 1 s
  excess, where weak approach and recede plateaus fall under `weak_db`.
  - Fuse the 3 s excess into the DP's columns on the plateaus: per column, the max of the 1 s excess and the 3 s
    excess resampled. Keep the 1 s excess near CPA, where the 3 s window smears about 7 Hz/s × 3 s.
  - It is a G1 candidate that reuses an arm that already works, and complements D2.
- **E3. Report detection as recall against band SNR** (F1), in every screen and decision run, and later on the
  4.5 recordings. Report spurious events per clip-minute alongside it.
- **E4. Arm b is the decision candidate.**
  - Arm a has no effect; drop it.
  - Arm c helps `loo:path` only and adds nothing on top of b. Keep it as a separate low-priority arm, not in
    the candidate.

## D1 + E1 diagnostics (session 7; reporting only, no knob)

**Method.** `reports/experiments/u19/d1.py` (summary: `d1_summary.py`; recall against SNR: `summarize.py`).
- Same units as the step-2 screen: six configs, seeds 0–49, scenario C, base and arm b, paired by audio.
- **Trace.** A traced copy of `fit.characterize_audio` records every peel attempt in every window (Hough seed,
  guided ridge, fit, descent check) and every post-fit removal. Its fits equal `fit.characterize_audio`'s on
  all 600 unit × arm runs, every field compared. `doppler/` is unchanged.
- **Recall reproduces the step-2 table exactly** in every config and arm.
- Rows stay in `outputs/` / scratch (screen rule).

**D1 classes** for a truth event with a window but no fit within 10 s, checked in this order:
1. *removed after fit*: an accepted fit within 10 s was removed (jet duplicate / harmonic of a prop fit, too
   slow, `dedupe_same_aircraft`);
2. *fit rejected*: a fit within 10 s, or on the event's curve, failed the descent check;
3. *fit mis-timed*: an accepted fit follows the event's curve but its t_cpa is more than 10 s off. "Follows"
   means at least half its ridge points lie within guide_hz·k of k·f_truth, for k a harmonic or half-order;
4. *no ridge*: none of the above. The trace records why the peel stopped in the event's window(s).

**E1 rungs** (one run per unit, base config):
- **R1, oracle windows.** One window per truth event, t_rx ± 30 s, clipped to the 60 s clip.
  - The windows go through the same automatic peel as detected windows. They are built with `manual=False`,
    because `detect.manual_windows` sets `manual=True`, which bypasses the descent check and swaps the band:
    that would change more than the window.
- **R2, oracle seed.** The R1 window; the guided ridge around the truth received-frequency curve (the
  harmonic or half-order line with the most columns ≥ `weak_db`); then the same fit and guided passes.
  - The truth curve leaves out the source-tone jitter / RPM walk. R2 can therefore miss a line that wanders
    more than guide_hz (3 Hz) from it, and does on some events the automatic path finds (R0 + R2 −: 6 all-on).
- **R3, truth ridge.** The truth curve itself (t_rx ± 60 s, 0.1 s step, 0.1 Hz noise, as
  `tests/test_cascade.ridge_events`), fitted by `fit_ridge`.
- These rungs are diagnostics, never a run claim.

### Reading

- **Detection.** On all-on, arm b already gets 82 of the 84 events that perfect windows would give. Its 1
  remaining window-less all-on miss (seed 13) would be fitted given a window.
  - The headroom left for a better detector is in `loo:path` (69 → 84) and `sub:reflection` (62 → 83).
  - D3 (Doppler-Hough detection) is therefore weakly motivated by all-on, the decision config.
- **The fit stage is not the problem.** Given the truth ridge it recovers 98 / 100 in every realistic config.
  - The 2 misses are the same two events in every config (seed 30 event 1, seed 48 event 0), 24–26 s off.
  - Both are scenario geometry (most likely a non-straight pass, the CLAUDE.md "turns and climbs" pitfall),
    not noise.
- **G1's column gate is not what loses the "window, no fit" events.** Along the truth curve, the *no ridge*
  misses have a median of 338 columns ≥ 12 dB (`weak_db`), against 50 needed; only 2 of 44 (base, all configs)
  fall below 50. Detected events: median 392.
  - The loss is the hand-off from the Hough seed to the guided ridge. The guided ridge takes the strongest
    bin within ±guide_hz (3 Hz) of the seed's straight-pass curve.
  - **Likely mechanism (an estimate from the grid steps, not measured):** the Hough grid is coarse (log-v step
    0.12, log-τ step 0.3, log-f step 0.008).
    - f0 rounding alone is ±0.4 %.
    - A half-step v error (±6 %) moves the plateaus by about ±0.06·v/c, roughly ±1.2 % at 70 m/s.
    - So on a harmonic at 200–400 Hz (R2 picks k ≥ 2 for most of these events), the seed curve can sit more
      than 3 Hz from the ridge over much of the pass, and fewer than 50 strong points fall inside the corridor.
    - The trace does not record the seed's offset from the ridge; that check comes first if this fix is built.
  - On a short guided ridge, `characterize_window` **breaks out of the peel for the whole window**: every
    other event in that window is lost too.
  - Of the 44 base *no ridge* misses (all configs), the seed lay on the missed event's curve in 25, and on
    something else in 19 (an event not yet peeled, then never revisited). In 26 of the 44 the same window had
    already yielded another fit.
- **D2 (path-level acceptance) is not what D1 points to.** It replaces the per-column gate, which holds here.
- **E2** (3 s excess fused into the DP) acts on the same gate, so it is not indicated either.
- **D1 points to a fix not on the list: the seed-to-ridge hand-off.**
  - For example, on a short guided ridge: mask the seed's family and `continue` the peel instead of `break`;
    or re-extract with the DP inside a corridor around the seed whose width follows the Hough grid steps.
  - R1 − R0 bounds what this fix can recover: on all-on, about 10 events with perfect windows. With arm b's
    windows, 13 all-on *no ridge* misses remain, because 3 events that b newly windows are lost here.
  - Building it needs the user's OK: it is not D2, E2 or D3.
- **F2 revised.** All-on base has 3 mis-timed fits, not 6:
  - seed 40: edge-clipped (coverage −0.1 / 1.0), v and d far off;
  - seed 47: a harmonic (k = 2) fitted 30 s late;
  - seed 48: the fit-stage geometry case above.
  The other spurious fits in those runs are not on a truth curve.
- **Too slow / jet duplicate** removals are rare: 1 all-on, at most 2 in any config.

**User decision (session 7):** the seed-to-ridge hand-off arm is approved as a task-4 candidate (it is not
D2, E2 or D3). Like every arm it comes after the decision run: registered in `reports/apriori_registry.md`
before its first measurement, off by default, and screened on the six configs, seeds 0–49, paired by audio.
Its first step is to measure what the trace leaves open: the seed curve's offset from the ridge in the
corridor.

## Step 3: pre-registered acceptance rule for arm b (written before the decision run)

**Arms.** `config.yaml` variants, added in this step so the config hash stays fixed from here on:
- **V1b** = V1 + `detect.long_window_s: 3.0`;
- **V0b** = V0 + the same.

The value 3.0 is the registry's a-priori midpoint of plan §8.2's 2–4 s (`reports/apriori_registry.md`); it is
not moved. No other knob changes. V0 and V1 stay runnable and compared.

**Decision run.**
- **When and where:** after this rule is approved and merged, from a clean `main`.
- **What:** all-on C, seeds 0–99, variants V0b and V1b:
  `cli.py ablate --plan stageA --limit 50` (all-on seeds 0–49) and `--plan stageA_ext` (seeds 50–99), with
  `--jobs 8` and the 900 s cap.
- **Pairing:** by seed with the reference rows already committed: V0 / V1 from Stage A (seeds 0–49) and U18
  (seeds 50–99), `reports/data/`.
- **Same machine:** timeouts are wall-clock (CONTRIBUTING §4). The run uses the compute machine that produced
  those rows, at the same `--jobs`.
- **Analysis:** fixed in advance in `reports/experiments/u19/decision.py`, written and tested before the run
  (on the reference rows renamed as an arm, where it reproduces the U18 table for V0 and V1 exactly and finds
  100 identical runs, no gain, and C2–C3 met).
- **Committed:** the rows, gzip-compressed, as `reports/data/u19_rows.jsonl.gz`.

**Validity checks. If either fails, stop and investigate before reading any criterion.**
- **V1, detection.** Detection does not depend on the cascade variant, so V0b's and V1b's rows must agree on
  every fitted event.
- **V2, code paths.** On runs whose fitted events equal the reference's (same counts and per-event residuals,
  exactly), V0b and V1b must reproduce V0's and V1's result fields exactly, timeouts aside. Arm b changes only
  detection rows. Any other difference means the V0 / V1 path changed and the pairing is invalid.

**Criteria. Arm b is accepted only if C1, C1b, C2 and C3 all hold.**

- **C1, recall goes up.** All-on, seeds 0–99, paired on truth events.
  - Events gained > events lost, with an exact two-sided sign test p < 0.05 on the discordant events.
  - Detection per event comes from the V0b / V0 rows, which are the variants without timeouts; the V1 rows
    are the fallback.
  - The test is two-sided at 0.05 as in U18. Why the event and not the run: arm b acts on events.
- **C1b, the unscreened half does not point the other way.** On seeds 50–99 alone: events gained ≥ events
  lost.
  - Why: seeds 0–49 were screened already (7 gained / 0 lost), so C1 is partly in-sample. Seeds 50–99 are
    fresh. Half the sample cannot carry a significance test of its own (6 / 0 is the smallest result with
    p < 0.05), so it only has to agree in direction.
- **C2, spurious events are not up by more than a quarter.** Over the 100 runs, the arm's total spurious
  events (`extra_events`, fitted events matching no truth event) ≤ 1.25 × the reference's.
  - From the committed reference rows, the V0 total on seeds 0–99 is 46 (0.46 per run; 0.52 on seeds 0–49),
    so the bound is 57 events.
  - Why 1.25: it is the upper limit of the conventional 0.80–1.25 equivalence range for a ratio, a margin
    fixed by convention, not by these data.
  - Why a ratio rather than a sign test: spurious counts are 0–2 per run, so a test has little power, and
    "no significant rise" would pass by default.
  - Why spurious matters: V1 absorbs at most one unmatched event per hypothesis, so each spurious event adds
    hypotheses and discrimination risk (plan §8.2). V0 rejects the true cell on any spurious event, which is
    why V0b's numbers are reported.
- **C3, rank-1 accuracy does not drop (non-inferiority).**
  - V1b's rank-1 accuracy, status ignored (right pair and ≤ 200 m), on all-on C n = 100 is at least V1's
    34 on the same seeds.
  - Why a point rule: F3 says detection can lift V1 by at most about 14 runs and arm b by a few, so a gain
    cannot be shown at n = 100.
  - It is the same "must not drop" reading as U18, where V1c failed 25 < 34.
  - Also reported: the paired sign test on discordant seeds, V1b-only right against V1-only right.
  - Known cost: timeout flips are wall-clock noise, so a harmless arm can fail C3 by a run or two. The V2
    check and the hand-check below show whether a drop comes from changed detections or from timeouts. The
    rule is not relaxed after the fact.

**Reported, not criteria** (plan §8.1, for V0, V0b, V1 and V1b):
- correct;
- rank-1 accuracy;
- answer rate;
- precision of unique with its 95 % interval;
- confident-wrong;
- timeouts;
- recall;
- the detection class;
- conditional rank-1 accuracy and precision on each arm's own fully-detected runs, and rank-1 accuracy on the
  runs fully detected under both V1 and V1b;
- paired tests: rank-1 accuracy and confident-wrong (sign), rank-1 error and true-assignment rank
  (Wilcoxon);
- recall against peak band SNR on seeds 0–99 (E3), with spurious per clip-minute;
- a hand-check of at least 5 seeds whose V1 rank-1 result changed, against their kept truth and events.

**If accepted.** Arm b becomes the candidate default `detect.long_window_s: 3.0`, for V0 and V1 alike. That
choice is the user's.
- **The code is unaffected:** V1b's code path equals V1's with one config value changed.
- **References:** the V0b / V1b rows become the new reference, and the Stage A / U18 rows stay the pre-U19
  reference.
- **Config hash:** a default change moves `config_sha256`. Later comparisons pair against the V0b / V1b rows,
  with that recorded.

**If rejected.** V1b and V0b stay comparison arms. The hand-off arm and any later detection arm are measured
against V1.

## Decision run, attempt 1 (session 8): invalid, arm b was never applied

**What ran.** V0b and V1b, all-on C seeds 0–99, from clean `main` at `ac88137`, `--jobs 8`, 900 s cap, on the
compute machine that produced the Stage A / U18 rows: 200 rows, about 56 min. `ac88137` is PR #8 merged on top
of the step-3 commit. PR #8's `diagnostics:` section is off by default and not on the ablation path, but it moved
`config_sha256` after step 3, which meant to keep it fixed.

**Why it is invalid.** `cli.py ablate` ran detection once per unit with the *base* config and handed those fits to
every variant (`_ablate_worker`: `run_events(..., cfg, c)`, then `run_locate(vcfg, inp, fits=fits, ...)`). A
variant's `detect:` overlay therefore never reached detection. V0b and V1b were V0 and V1 under other names:
- fitted events identical to the reference on all 100 runs (the V2 count), recall 156 / 200 in both, 0 events
  gained or lost;
- spurious events 46 in both;
- V1b 35 against V1 34: one timeout flip (seed 23), the only changed V1 result.

This is the pattern `decision.py` produced in its own self-test on the reference rows renamed as an arm. The
pre-registered validity checks V1 and V2 both pass on it: they test that the arm changed *only* detection, not
that it changed detection at all. **No criterion is read from this run, and the "REJECT" it printed is void.**

The rows are not committed (they are not a decision result); they stay in `outputs/ablation/u19/`.

**What is unaffected.**
- The step-2 screen and D1 / E1: `screen.py` and `d1.py` call `run_events` with each arm's own config.
- U18 (V1c / V0c): their overlays change scoring and ambiguity only, so detection sharing was correct there.
  `test_detection_key_follows_detection_sections_only` checks that V0, V1, V0c and V1c detect like the base.

**Fix** (branch `s8-u19-decision`):
- `cli.py`: `detection_key(cfg)`, a hash of the config sections `run_events` reads (`spectrogram`,
  `spectrogram_jet`, `detect`, `ridge`, `ridge_jet`, `fit`, `classify`, `environment`).
  - `variant_fits` detects once per distinct key in a unit, so V0b / V1b share one detection and V0 / V1 another.
  - Each variant's own events are kept as `events.parquet` (base) or `events_<key>.parquet`. The summary's
    detection recount reads the variant's own file.
  - Every row records `detection_sha256`.
- `decision.py`: a new validity check **V0, arm applied**. Every non-timeout V0b / V1b row must record the
  arm's own detection key, which must differ from V1's. Rows without the record, including all of attempt 1's,
  fail, and the script stops before any criterion. This adds a refusal only; C1–C3, their numbers and the other
  checks are unchanged.
- Tests: the key per variant, detection under each variant's own config, the per-variant recount, and V0.

**Check of the fix** (a screen from the branch worktree, not a decision result): all-on C seeds 0–15, V0 and V0b.
- V0's 16 rows equal Stage A's on every compared field, including `per_event`.
- V0b's detection differs on 9 of 16 seeds: truth events detected 23 → 26, spurious 8 → 6.

## Task 4, step 1: where the seed-to-ridge hand-off fails (session 8; diagnostics, no knob)

**Method.**
- `reports/experiments/u19/handoff_offset.py` (summary: `handoff_offset_summary.py`) runs d1.py's traced peel at
  the current default detection (arm b).
- Six configs, seeds 0–49, scenario C: 300 units. The trace's fits equal `fit.characterize_audio`'s on all 300.
- Per prop-band peel attempt with a Hough seed:
  - the truth line nearest the seed curve: the event and the harmonic / half-order k minimising the median
    relative offset;
  - the offset in Hz and in Hough grid steps;
  - on a short guided ridge (`guided_short`), the strong columns (≥ `weak_db`) in the ±`guide_hz` corridor of
    the masked and unmasked excess, and at 0.5, 1.5, 2 and 3 × the seed line.
- Rows stay in scratch (screen rule).

**The seed is on target; D1's grid-step guess is refuted.**
- 50 missed truth events have a short guided ridge on their line. For 34 of them a seed sat within 5 % of the
  event's line: 39 attempts.
- Those seeds are as accurate as the ones that become fits:

| attempt (seed within 5 % of a truth line) | n | k = 1 | median \|Δf\| Hz | frames within ±3 Hz | \|f0\| / \|v\| / \|τ\| grid steps (medians) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| became a fit | 551 | 486 | 0.68 | 1.00 | 1.5 / 0.9 / 0.7 |
| short guided ridge, event missed | 39 | 33 | 0.73 | 1.00 | 1.4 / 0.9 / 0.9 |

A corridor that follows the grid steps would not recover these events.

**What does lose them: the seed's own line is weak, its harmonics are strong.**

| short guided ridge | n | seed line (median) | strong columns at 1× seed: masked / unmasked | at 2× / 3× seed (masked) | a harmonic ≥ min_points (50) | unmasked excess gives a ridge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| missed event, seed on its line | 39 | 117 Hz | 11 / 16 | 258 / 120 | **39** | 3 |
| missed event, seed elsewhere | 16 | 62 Hz | 8 / 12 | 54 / 0 | 8 | 0 |
| event detected anyway | 202 | 97 Hz | 10 / 14 | 22 / 3 | 117 | 13 |

- The Hough seed sums `hough_harmonics` = 3 harmonics, so it finds a source whose fundamental is weak from its
  2nd and 3rd harmonics.
- `guided_ridge` then follows only the fundamental, finds fewer than 50 strong points, and the peel `break`s
  out of the window.
- Earlier masks are not the cause: masked and unmasked counts differ by a few columns.
- The 16 "elsewhere" missed events are lost to the `break` itself: their window's seed lay on another line,
  after a fit had already been peeled in every case.
- The third row is the risk. In 117 short guided ridges whose event was detected anyway, nearly all after an
  earlier fit (199 / 202), a harmonic of the seed also holds enough strong points. A hand-off there makes an
  extra fit. Such extra fits next to a truth CPA were the mechanism behind arm b's three V1 losses.

**Proposed arm h (not yet registered or measured; needs the user's OK, since it is not the corridor version
named in session 7):**
- **h1, harmonic hand-off** (`ridge.handoff_harmonics: true`). On a short guided ridge, try the guided ridge
  around m × the seed line, for m = 2 … `hough_harmonics`, the harmonics the seed statistic already sums. Take
  the one with the most points, then fit and run the guided passes as usual.
  - No new number. The jet band has `hough_harmonics: 1`, so it is unchanged.
- **h2, continue** (`ridge.handoff_continue: true`). If no ridge results, mask the seed's family and `continue`
  the peel instead of `break`. It stays bounded by the existing `max_ridges + 2` attempts. No new number.
- **h12** = both. All are off by default, so V0 and V1 are unchanged.
- **Screen:** h1, h2 and h12 against the current default, six configs, seeds 0–49, paired by audio. Measures:
  - recall and spurious events per run;
  - extra fits near a truth CPA: unmatched fits within `evaluate.match_tol_s` of a truth CPA, the arm-b loss
    mechanism;
  - matched-fit errors |Δv| / v and |Δd| / d (medians);
  - per-unit detection time.
- **Rule for the screen:** it can only rule an arm out. An arm that survives goes to a pre-registered decision
  run as arm b did.
