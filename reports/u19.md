# U19: detection arms (plan §8.2), session 6

## Step 1: the arms

Built in `doppler/detect.py`, off by default (V0 / V1 unchanged), registered in `reports/apriori_registry.md`
before any measurement:
- **(a)** a harmonic-summed tonal row: per frame, the max over fundamentals of the mean excess at f, 2f, 3f;
- **(b)** the prop bands on a 3 s long-window spectrogram;
- **(c)** a separate 20–100 Hz row, below the recording high-pass corner.

No existing threshold moved. Each arm only adds rows to the max-over-rows statistic.

## Step 2: detection-only screen (seeds 0–49)

**Method.** `reports/experiments/u19/screen.py` (summary: `summarize.py`). Each unit rebuilds the scenario as
`cli.py ablate` does, then runs `cli.run_events` (detection, ridge, fit; no cascade) once per arm on the same
audio, so every comparison is paired by seed and by audio.
- **Recall** is the Stage A definition: a truth event counts if a fitted event lies within 10 s of its received
  CPA time.
- **Spurious** = fitted events that match no truth event.
- Configs: clean, all-on, `loo:path`, `loo:levels`, `loo:scene`, `sub:reflection` (§8.4), seeds 0–49 each.
- It is a screen: rows stay in `outputs/`, not committed.

**Checks.**
- The base arm reproduces the detection counts recorded in the Stage A and U18 rows on all 125 overlapping
  units.
- The base arm after the arm code went in matches the base screen run before it on all 300 units, every field.

**Reading.**
- **Arm b (3 s long window) is the only arm with a clear effect.** It gains 7 all-on events and loses none
  (p = 0.016), with similar gains in `loo:path` (+13) and `sub:reflection` (+10). Spurious events do not go up:
  all-on 0.52 → 0.42 per run, 2 runs up and 6 down.
- **Arm a (harmonic sum) does nothing measurable** on all-on. On the test fixture its gain was only +1.5 dB:
  the realism layer's harmonics fall at −3 dB per order, so the mean over three harmonics is weaker than the
  fundamental.
- **Arm c (low band) helps only `loo:path`** (+6). Adding it to b gains nothing over b alone (abc ≈ b).
- **Where the gains come from.** All of b's all-on gains are events that never got a window: 5 below the
  trigger, 2 above it nearby. **None of the 14 all-on misses lost after detection** (a window, but no fitted
  event) is recovered. They are now the largest all-on loss and lie outside §8.2's candidates. They need a
  ridge / fit look.
- **Effect on accuracy.** Recall only bounds accuracy. More detections also mean more hypotheses and timeouts;
  only the decision run can show whether V1's rank-1 accuracy rises.

## Brainstorm after the screen (session 6): what the screen adds to `docs/u19_detection_literature.md`

The literature note's D1–D5 stand. These points come from the screen rows and the U18 rows, with no
re-run, and sharpen them.

**Findings**
- **F1. The cliff sits at about 6 dB band SNR.** Recall by the event's peak band SNR, pooled over the six
  configs:

  | band SNR (dB) | < 1 | 1–2 | 2–3 | 3–4 | 4–6 | 6–8 | ≥ 8 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | base | 0.52 | 0.59 | 0.69 | 0.66 | 0.67 | 1.00 | 0.97 |
  | arm b | 0.64 | 0.68 | 0.70 | 0.77 | 0.76 | 1.00 | 0.98 |

  322 of the 600 events lie below 6 dB, because the level model resamples events to ≥ 0 dB
  (`realism.event_min_snr_db`). Recall on the synthetic mix therefore mostly measures the generator's SNR
  distribution. **The transferable quantity is the recall-against-SNR curve**, which can be compared with
  field recordings.
- **F2. Some lost-after-detection misses look mis-timed, not lost.** Of the 14 all-on events that got a window
  but no matched fit, 6 sit in runs that also have a spurious fit: probably a fit whose t_cpa is more than 10 s
  from the truth (D1 cause 3). The other 8 have no fit near them at all (causes 1, 2, 4 or 5).
- **F3. Detection caps accuracy less than recall suggests.** On U18's all-on C n = 100, V1 has the right answer
  in 31 of the 65 fully detected runs, but in only 3 of the 35 runs that missed an event. **Perfect detection
  would lift V1's rank-1 accuracy (status ignored) from 34 to about 48 at most**, if those runs then behaved
  like the fully detected ones. Arm b recovers 7 of 25 all-on events, so expect a few runs, which a paired test
  at n = 100 cannot confirm. The acceptance rule should not hinge on a rank-1 gain. Discrimination (34 of the
  65 fully detected runs still fail) remains the larger lever.

**Additions to D1–D5**
- **E1. Oracle-window ladder (reporting only).**
  - Give the ridge and fit stage the truth event windows as manual windows. This is a diagnostic: never a run
    claim, and recorded as a manual input.
  - Then give the fit truth-derived ridge points (`tests/test_cascade.ridge_events`).
  - The two rungs bound what detection fixes (D3) and ridge fixes (D2) can achieve, and finish D1's breakdown
    in one screen.
- **E2. Carry arm b into the ridge stage.** Arm b only adds detection rows. The ridge DP still runs on the 1 s
  excess, where weak approach and recede plateaus fall under `weak_db`.
  - Fuse the 3 s excess into the DP's columns on the plateaus: per column, the max of the 1 s excess and the 3 s
    excess resampled. Keep the 1 s excess near CPA, where the 3 s window smears about 7 Hz/s × 3 s.
  - It is a G1 candidate that reuses an arm that already works, and complements D2.
- **E3. Report detection as recall against band SNR** (F1), in every screen and decision run, and later on the
  4.5 recordings. Report spurious events per clip-minute alongside it.
- **E4. Arm b is the decision candidate.**
  - Arm a has no effect; drop it.
  - Arm c helps `loo:path` only and adds nothing on top of b. Keep it as a separate low-priority arm, not in
    the candidate.
